# AEP From Zero: Adobe Experience Platform Full Course (free)

**Adobe Experience Platform (AEP), Real-Time CDP, Adobe Journey Optimizer (AJO) and Customer Journey Analytics (CJA), from zero to job-ready.**
A free 37-episode story course on the iTV YouTube channel by Surge Software Solutions, Bengaluru, with reading notes, slides,
exercises with answers, and sample data for every episode.

**Watch the full playlist:** https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLBsYB0qLaFoc
  
**60-second Shorts:** https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLLKdmtklg1io

## The story
Kavya, a new marketing technologist, joins **Bloom**, a direct-to-consumer wellness brand with 1.2 million customers spread across
three systems that do not talk to each other. Episode by episode her team builds one real-time customer profile, governed audiences,
personalised journeys, offer decisioning, cross-channel analytics and an AI layer, then presents it all on Demo Day.
Bloom, its people and its customers are fictional.

## How to use this course
1. Watch the episodes in order. Each one is about 15 minutes and builds on the last.
2. After each episode, read its notes and do the exercise (answers are at the bottom, hidden until you click).
3. No Adobe access? Every exercise has a track you can do on paper. Have a sandbox? Follow the read-only track. Never send to real customers.
4. Use the [sample data](data/) wherever an episode loads files.

## The four parts
- **Adobe Real-Time CDP and AEP Tutorial From Zero (Part 1)** (episodes 1-15): Free Adobe Experience Platform and Real-Time CDP tutorial from zero, part 1 of AEP From Zero: schemas, ingestion, identity, profiles, audiences, activation, governance, APIs and Query Service. Bloom goes from three disconnected systems to one real-time customer profile.
- **Adobe Journey Optimizer Tutorial From Zero (AJO, Part 2)** (episodes 16-24): Free Adobe Journey Optimizer (AJO) tutorial from zero, part 2 of AEP From Zero: campaigns and journeys, testing safely, fragments, personalisation, abandoned cart, offer decisioning, guardrails and journey troubleshooting. Bloom learns to speak to each customer at the right moment.
- **Customer Journey Analytics Tutorial From Zero (CJA, Part 3)** (episodes 25-32): Free Customer Journey Analytics (CJA) tutorial from zero, part 3 of AEP From Zero: connections, data views, derived fields, Analysis Workspace, segments, calculated metrics, attribution, fallout, flow, publishing audiences and Adobe Analytics vs CJA. Bloom proves what worked.
- **AI for Adobe Marketers: AI Assistant, RAG and Agents (Part 4)** (episodes 33-37): AI for Adobe Experience Cloud teams, part 4 of AEP From Zero: AI Assistant and Coworker, RAG on your own data, AI agents and MCP, then the capstone demo and interview questions. Bloom's small team works like a big one.
