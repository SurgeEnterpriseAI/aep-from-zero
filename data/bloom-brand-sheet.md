# Bloom brand sheet (sample)

Bloom's tone guide for every email, push message and content card. Owner: Divya Nair, CRM and journeys lead. Bloom, its people and its address are fictional; this file is practice material for the AEP From Zero course (episodes 19 and 33).

## Logo

- The wordmark is **bloom**, in lower case, set in Leaf green, with a small five-petal flower before the first letter.
- Place it on Cream or white, never on a busy photo, and never stretch or recolour it.
- The logo image itself is not part of this sample; use the word "bloom" as a stand-in when you practise.

## Tone rules

1. **Warm and plain.** Write like a friend who knows skincare: short sentences, everyday words.
2. **No shouting.** No whole words in capital letters, and no strings of exclamation marks.
3. **No false urgency.** Never invent a deadline, a countdown or a "last chance".
4. **No promise the product cannot keep.** No guaranteed results, no timelines for results, and no health or medical claims.
5. **Only real offers.** Name only the approved offers: the 20% Comeback (Lapsed 90 only), the Early Access Drop (Gold only) and Free Shipping (the fallback). Bloom's win-back offer is the 20% Comeback. Never invent a discount.
6. **Greet by first name, with a fallback.** If the first name is empty, write "Hi there", never "Hi ,".
7. **Respect consent.** Marketing messages go only to people who agreed to hear from Bloom, and every marketing email carries the footer below.

## The legal block

The Bloom footer holds this text, followed by one word, Unsubscribe:

> Bloom, 1 Example Road, Bengaluru, India. You are receiving this email because you agreed to hear from Bloom.

The address is fictional.

## Approvals

A person approves every line that goes to customers, including lines an AI tool drafted. For Bloom's brand voice that person is Divya. The approver, not the tool, is accountable.
